Welcome To Study Hours Performance Predictor

Welcome to the Student Pass Prediction System, an innovative web application powered by Machine Learning and Logistic Regression that helps predict student academic performance with accuracy and speed. This platform analyzes important factors such as attendance, study hours, marks, and assignment performance to determine whether a student is likely to pass or fail. Designed with a user-friendly interface, the system provides instant predictions that can assist students, teachers, and educational institutions in making smarter academic decisions and improving learning outcomes.
